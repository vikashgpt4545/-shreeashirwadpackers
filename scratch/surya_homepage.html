Ranchi's Trusted Packers and Movers Since 2011

Surya Packers & Movers was founded by Ranjeet Chaudhary with a simple promise: honest pricing, careful hands, and a phone number you can actually reach. For more than a decade, we have operated from our head office on NH-33 (Ranchi–Patna Road), near Choudhary Petrol Pump, Booty, Dumardaga – a real office you can walk into, with trucks you can see and a team you can meet before you hand over your belongings.

We are not a broker platform that subcontracts your move to whoever is cheapest that day. Every move is surveyed, packed and dispatched by our own trained team, with written estimates before work begins and itemised invoices after. That is why thousands of Ranchi families, professionals and businesses have relied on us across the city and to every major city in India.

We are MSME and GST registered, and every move comes with a GST invoice, packing list and consignment note – the documents employers ask for when processing transfer allowance claims.

Every Ranchi Locality – Doorstep Crews

Ranchi is our home. From Booty More on NH-33 to Hatia in the south and Kanke in the north-west, our packing crews reach every locality in the city. Local knowledge matters more than most people realise: Ranchi has apartment societies with fixed shifting hours, narrow lanes in Old Ranchi that need compact vehicles, and monsoon-season challenges on hillside colonies that only an experienced local team handles smoothly.

We cover all major localities including Bariatu, Kanke, Lalpur, Doranda, Harmu, Hatia, Morabadi, Kokar, Ratu Road and Booty More – and the wider Ranchi district including Namkum, Tupudana and Ormanjhi on request. See our full Ranchi localities guide for the complete locality index.

All-India Routes from Our Ranchi Head Office

Our office on the Ranchi–Patna highway is the launching point for one of Jharkhand’s busiest intercity moving corridors. We run regular full-truck and part-load departures to Delhi (via NH-33/NH-19), Mumbai, Kolkata (just 400 km away), Bangalore, Patna, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Lucknow, Jamshedpur and Dhanbad. Part-load sharing cuts the cost of a 1RK or 1BHK intercity move by 30–50%, while full-truck gives the fastest delivery for larger homes or time-sensitive transfers.

Every intercity consignment receives a packing list, a consignment copy, and WhatsApp updates during transit. Car and bike transport on all routes is documented with photo condition reports at pickup and delivery, and GST invoices are issued for employer reimbursement. Visit our all-India relocation page or jump directly to a route: Ranchi to Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bangalore, Patna.

FAQ

Common Questions Answered

How much do packers and movers charge in Ranchi?

Local shifting within Ranchi typically costs Rs 4,000 to Rs 18,000 depending on home size and distance, while moves from Ranchi to metro cities range from Rs 14,000 to Rs 55,000. Surya Packers gives a fixed written quote after a free survey, so there are no surprises on moving day.

Do you provide packing material?

Yes, all packing material is included free in the quoted price – corrugated boxes, bubble wrap, foam sheets, stretch film, waterproof lamination for mattresses and wooden crating for TVs, glass and marble items.

Which areas of Ranchi do you cover?

We cover all of Ranchi including Booty, Bariatu, Kanke, Lalpur, Doranda, Harmu, Hatia, Morabadi, Kokar and Ratu Road, plus intercity moves to any city in India.

How do I book a move with Surya Packers?

Call or WhatsApp 94301 10339, or submit the quote form on this page. We do a quick survey (phone, video or in person), share a written estimate within 30 minutes, and lock your moving date.

Can you transport my car or bike from Ranchi?

Yes. We transport cars on closed or open carriers and bikes with foam wrap and optional wooden crating. Photo condition reports are taken at pickup and delivery on every vehicle move.

Do you offer part-load shipping to other cities?

Yes. Part-load (shared truck) options are available on all major routes from Ranchi, which cuts cost by 30–50% for smaller moves like 1RK or 1BHK.

Is there an office I can visit in Ranchi?

Yes – NH-33, Ranchi–Patna Rd, near Choudhary Petrol Pump, Krishna Pura, Booty, Dumardaga, Ranchi 834012. Open 8 AM to 9 PM, all 7 days.
