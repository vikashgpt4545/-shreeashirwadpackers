Rigorous 5-Layer Packaging Standard

To eliminate any possibility of cargo damage over high-speed expressway transit, our skilled packaging crews follow a rigorous 5-layer methodology:

  1. Surface Film: Clean stretch wrap shields all polished wood, upholstery, and leather from dust, moisture, and friction.
  2. Shock Barrier: Thick bubble wrap encases crockery, glass tabletops, wall art, and electronic equipment.
  3. Corrugated Armour: Heavy-duty 5-ply cardboard sheeting wrapped along furniture surfaces and appliance bodies.
  4. Edge Reinforcement: Compressed cardboard corner profiles shield vulnerable wardrobe and table edges from side collisions.
  5. Outer Casing: High-tensile strapping bands and waterproof tarpaulins secure boxes firmly inside vehicle compartments.
