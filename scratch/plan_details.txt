#### Group 1: New Standalone Intercity Corridors (2,767 Keywords &rarr; ~350 Master Pages)
* **What these keywords do**: These are long-distance moving queries where customers are physically relocating from one city to another across state borders.
* **Why standalone pages are mandatory**: Google treats a search like `hyderabad to bangalore packers and movers` as an entirely different search intent from a local shifting query. Customers want route distance, transit timelines (e.g. NH-44, 12 to 18 hours), octroi/toll checkpoints, and dedicated container truck availability.
* **Top Search Corridors in the Database**:
  - **Bangalore Corridors (500+ queries)**: `bangalore to hyderabad` (91 queries), `bangalore to mumbai` (45 queries), `bangalore to delhi` (40 queries), `bangalore to chennai` (36 queries), `bangalore to pune` (21 queries), `bangalore to kolkata` (13 queries).
  - **Hyderabad Corridors (450+ queries)**: `hyderabad to bangalore` (94 queries), `hyderabad to chennai` (16 queries), `hyderabad to pune` (14 queries), `hyderabad to delhi` (11 queries), `hyderabad to vijayawada` (10 queries), `hyderabad to mumbai` (8 queries).
  - **Mumbai & Pune Corridors (400+ queries)**: `pune to mumbai` (52 queries), `mumbai to pune` (44 queries), `mumbai to bangalore` (66 queries), `mumbai to delhi` (18 queries), `delhi to pune` (17 queries), `pune to hyderabad` (14 queries).
  - **Delhi & North Corridors (350+ queries)**: `delhi to bangalore` (57 queries), `delhi to mumbai` (15 queries), `delhi to patna` (12 queries), `delhi to jaipur` (10 queries), `delhi to kolkata` (9 queries).
  - **Chennai & South Corridors (300+ queries)**: `chennai to bangalore` (47 queries), `chennai to hyderabad` (23 queries), `chennai to coimbatore` (20 queries), `chennai to nagercoil` (8 queries).
* **Consolidation Rule**: All sub-variations (e.g., `hyderabad to bangalore car transport`, `cost of shifting from hyderabad to bangalore`, `household luggage hyderabad to bangalore`) are mapped to the single master corridor URL: `pages/hyderabad-to-bangalore-packers-and-movers.php`.

#### Group 3: In-Content Keywords for Existing City Hub Pages (5,437 Keywords)
* **What these keywords do**: These are authority and intent modifiers attached to city searches (e.g., `best packers in [city]`, `top 10 movers in [city]`, `cheap packers and movers in [city]`, `IBA approved movers in [city]`, `packers and movers near me`).
* **Why these MUST NEVER be separate pages**:
  - Creating `best-packers-and-movers-in-bangalore.php` alongside `packers-and-movers-in-bangalore.php` is textbook **Keyword Cannibalization**. Google sees them as doorway duplicates and deranks both.
* **How they are deployed**:
  - **H2 Headings**: *"Why Shree Ashirwad is Rated the Best Packers and Movers in [City]"*.
  - **Trust & Verification Badges**: *"IBA Approved & Government Registered Household Shifting"*.
  - **Schema Questions**: *"How do I find verified and cheap packers and movers near me?"*.
  - **Meta Tags**: Included directly in the `<meta name="keywords">` attribute of the existing city hub.
